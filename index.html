About

Made by a trader who kept forgetting

Trade Buddy started with one problem: after setting a bias and waiting for a POI, it was easy to lose track of which pairs were analysed and which were entered. A simple tracker fixed that, and it grew into a full toolkit for disciplined traders.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is Trade Buddy free?

You can start free. If paid features arrive later, we will say so clearly first.

Does it place trades for me?

No. Trade Buddy is a planning and review tool. You trade on your own broker or platform.

Where is my data kept?

Your setups are saved to your account and on your device, and you can back them up at any time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It is designed phone-first and runs in any modern browser.
