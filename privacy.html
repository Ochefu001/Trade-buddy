For the purposes of data protection law, we are the “data controller” of the personal information described below.

2. What Trade Buddy does

Trade Buddy lets you record trades, plan trade setups, see statistics such as win rate and risk-to-reward, use calculators, and receive notifications. It does not place trades, hold money, or connect to your brokerage account. A connection to MT4/MT5 is planned but is not available. If it is added, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission before any such connection.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us when you sign up

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your password. It is handled by Firebase Authentication, which stores it in a protected (hashed) form. We cannot see or recover your password.
  • Your agreement to our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, recorded with the document version numbers and the time you agreed.

Account information we store

  • A unique account ID, the date your account was created, and whether your email address is verified.
  • Your display name, which you can change in Profile.
  • Notifications we send to your account (for example a welcome message or an announcement) and whether you have read them.

Trading information you choose to enter

  • Trade records: instrument or pair, buy or sell direction, result (win, loss or break-even), risk size in R, amount made or lost in dollars, trade date, how you felt, whether you followed your plan, and your notes.
  • Trade setups: instrument, bias, timeframes, your notes, entry zone, status (waiting, at entry zone, entered, completed) and your comments.

Entering trading information is optional and under your control. Please do not put passwords, bank details, ID numbers or other sensitive details in your notes. Trade Buddy does not need them.

Technical information

When you use the website, technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the time of requests is processed by the services that deliver the site. These are our hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages; we may move to another host, such as Truehost, and will update this page if we do), Google Firebase, and Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces. Firebase Authentication also keeps a small sign-in record in your browser so you stay logged in.

The current version of Trade Buddy does not include our own analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment card details, bank details, or your broker login details. There are no payments in Trade Buddy at this time.

5. How your records are stored

Your information is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, which are Firebase services run by Google. Each person’s records are stored under their own account ID. Database security rules are set so that a signed-in user can read and change only their own records. See Your Trading Data: How It Works for a plain-language explanation. Our Firestore database location is [FIRESTORE REGION, shown in Firebase Console under Firestore].

6. Who can access your information

  • You. You can see and edit your own records.
  • Other Trade Buddy users: no. The database rules do not allow one user to read another user’s records.
  • Administrators. A small number of authorized administrators can sign in to an admin dashboard. It shows account names, email addresses, sign-up dates and summaries of trades and setups (for example counts, results and instruments). The database rules allow administrators to read user records, including trade and setup records, so in principle that includes your notes. Administrators cannot edit or delete your trades or setups, and we use this access only to run, support, secure and improve the service. Administrators can send notifications to your account.
  • Service providers. Google (Firebase and Google Fonts) and our hosting provider process information on our behalf to run the service.

9. How we protect your information

  • Connections to Trade Buddy and Firebase use HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Google states that Firebase and Cloud Firestore encrypt data at rest. See Google’s documentation for details.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not visible to us.
  • Database rules limit users to their own records, and administrator access is limited to named administrator accounts that can be set up only inside our Firebase project.
  • Account deletion requires you to confirm your password first.

No online service can be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep it private. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will take the steps the law requires, which may include notifying you and the regulator.

10. How long we keep information, and deleting it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself: go to Dashboard → Menu → Data & Privacy → Delete account (also reachable from Profile). After you confirm with your password, we delete your trades, setups, notifications, profile record, consent record and sign-in account. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Some exceptions can apply. We may need to keep limited information if the law requires it, or to handle a legal claim, a dispute or a security investigation.
