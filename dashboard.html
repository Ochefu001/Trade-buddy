Account safety (risk of ruin)

The chance of losing a set share of your account if you keep trading this way. Lower is safer.

-Fill in the fields above.

A rough estimate. It assumes every trade risks the same percentage and trades are independent. Real results are usually a bit worse.

Expected profit per trade

On average, how much you make or lose each time you trade. A positive number means your strategy has an edge. (Also called expectancy.)

-(win rate × avg win) − (loss rate × avg loss)
