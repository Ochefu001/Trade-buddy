No payment or subscription data exists. Your current Firebase project stores users, trades, setups and notifications only. A user's account is not treated as a payment, and no revenue numbers are shown.

To enable this section later you will need:

1A payment provider (e.g. Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe)
2A trusted backend (Cloud Function) to confirm payments
3A write-only-by-backend "subscriptions" collection

Once that exists, this page can show subscribers, free vs paid, expired plans and revenue trends.
