Your Trading Data: How It Works

Plain-language guide · Last updated [EFFECTIVE DATE]
Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceYour Trading Data

You type trades and setups into Trade Buddy, and this page explains, in plain language, what happens to them next. For the full legal detail, read the Privacy Policy.

A. Where your records are stored

Trade Buddy uses Google Firebase. Your sign-in (email and password) is kept in Firebase Authentication. Your profile, trades, setups and notifications are kept in Cloud Firestore, Google’s online database. Every record is filed under your own account ID, so the app knows it is yours and shows it to you on any phone or computer where you sign in.

Your records are not kept only in your browser. If you clear your browser or change phones, they are still there when you sign in again.

B. Who can see your records

YesYou

You can see, edit and delete your own trades and setups.

NoOther users

The database rules do not let one user read another user’s records.

Administrators. A small number of authorized administrators can use an admin dashboard. It shows account names, emails, sign-up dates and summaries of trades and setups (for example how many, wins and losses, and popular instruments). The database rules allow administrators to read user records, so in principle that includes the notes you write. Administrators cannot edit or delete your trades and setups. We use this access only to run, support, secure and improve Trade Buddy.

Google and our hosting provider also process data in order to run the service, as explained in the Privacy Policy. We cannot promise absolute secrecy: no online system is perfectly secure. We do apply the protections described in the Privacy Policy.

C. How your records are used

The figures you see are calculated from what you entered:

  • Trade history: a searchable list of your trades.
  • Performance statistics: total results and averages.
  • Win rate: how many of your trades were wins.
  • Risk and reward: how big your wins are compared with your losses, shown in R.
  • Insights: patterns, such as how your mood or sticking to your plan relates to results.
  • Setup tracking: trade ideas that are waiting, at the entry zone, entered or completed.
  • Personal progress: how your results and habits change over time.

These are tools to help you reflect. They are not financial advice.

D. What Trade Buddy does and does not do

  • NoIt does not place or execute trades for you.
  • NoIt does not connect to your brokerage account today.
  • NoIt does not place orders.
  • NoIt does not send your trading records to brokers.
  • NoIt does not sell your trading records.
  • PlannedMT4/MT5 connection is planned and is not available yet. If it is added, we will update our policies first and ask for your permission before you connect anything.

Some pages link to broker and prop-firm websites. Some of those are affiliate links. Opening one takes you away from Trade Buddy, and we do not pass your trading records to those sites.

E. Your control over your records

  • View: My Trades, My Setups and Insights show everything you have entered.
  • Change: open any trade or setup to edit it, or change your name in Profile.
  • Delete single records: you can delete individual trades and setups.
  • Delete everything: Dashboard → Menu → Data & Privacy → Delete account permanently removes your trades, setups, notifications, profile, consent record and sign-in account. You will be asked for your password first. It cannot be undone.
  • Ask us: to request a copy of your information or ask any question, email [CONTACT EMAIL].
